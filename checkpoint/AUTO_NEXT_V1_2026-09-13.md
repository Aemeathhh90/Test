## Technical intent
When Auto Next is enabled and the current Media3 player reaches `STATE_ENDED`, invoke the existing `onNextEpisode` callback once. A per-video guard prevents duplicate callbacks while the player remains ended. When the `videoUrl` changes, the guard resets for the new episode.
